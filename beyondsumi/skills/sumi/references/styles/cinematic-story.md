# Cinematic Story
Visual identity for emotional storytelling and motivational journeys.

## Visual Theme
Full-color narrative environments, cinematic lighting & depth.

## Character DNA
Minimalist 2D animated stick figure wearing a bright red beanie (smooth rounded knit, no pom-pom) and a yellow t-shirt, with simple black stick limbs and shorts. Simple black lines, vibrant colors, smooth 2D animation style.

## Environment Specification
In a rich full-color cinematic environment [describe setting: e.g., a dimly lit cozy bedroom with warm lamplight / a vast open desert highway under a glowing dusk sky / a dramatic mountain ridge above rolling golden clouds]. Cinematic volumetric lighting, soft depth of field, atmospheric narrative mood.

## Negative Constraints
No photorealistic human skin or faces, no 3D humanoid CGI uncanny valley models, no chaotic line glitches, no speech bubbles, no dialogue text boxes.
