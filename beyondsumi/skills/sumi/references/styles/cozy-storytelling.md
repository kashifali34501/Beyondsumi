# Cozy Storytelling
Visual identity for relatable life stories and educational content.

## Visual Theme
Full-color 2D cartoon locations, gentle atmospheric light, varied cinematic shots.

## Character DNA
White filled round head and slim torso, dark stick limbs, simple expressive face.

## Guidelines
Treat locations as story beats, not one universal room. Use close action inserts, wide establishing views, lateral tracking and motivated location cuts.
