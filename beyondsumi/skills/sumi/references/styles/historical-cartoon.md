# Historical Cartoon
Visual identity for history explainers and civilization stories.

## Visual Theme
Full-color painterly historical settings, clean cel shading, era-specific wardrobe and props.

## Character DNA
White filled round heads with connected short white necks, expressive individual faces, solid black oval eyes, thin black exposed limbs and small solid black mitten-shaped palms continuous with the wrists, one subtle integrated thumb lobe, no individual fingers, finger separations, white fills or gloves.

## Guidelines
Freeze the drawing grammar; change the era pack, not the character rendering system. Use era-specific palette and story-appropriate music.
